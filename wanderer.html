"The Wanderer Above the Sea of Fog"

Painting Information

  • Artist: Caspar David Friedrich
  • Year Painted: 1818
  • Medium: Oil on canvas
  • Art Movement: Romanticism
  • Location: Hamburger Kunsthalle, Hamburg, Germany

About the Painting

The Wanderer Above the Sea of Fog is one of the most famous paintings by German Romantic artist Caspar David Friedrich. The painting shows a man standing on a rocky mountain peak while looking over a vast landscape covered by fog. The mountains and clouds create a dramatic and mysterious atmosphere. The figure is shown from behind, allowing the viewer to imagine themselves standing in the same position and looking at the landscape. The painting is often associated with ideas of nature, reflection, exploration, and the individual's place within the natural world.

About the Artist

Caspar David Friedrich was a German Romantic landscape painter. He was known for creating dramatic landscapes that often explored themes of nature, solitude, spirituality, and human reflection.
