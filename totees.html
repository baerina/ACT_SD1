"Totes Meer"

Painting Information

  • Artist: Paul Nash
  • Year Painted: 1940–1941
  • Medium: Oil on canvas
  • Art Movement: Modernism
  • Location: Tate Britain, London

About the Painting

Totes Meer, which means "Dead Sea" in German, is a painting by British artist Paul Nash. The painting shows the remains of crashed and damaged aircraft gathered together like waves of a sea. Nash created the painting during World War II to show the destruction caused by war. The twisted aircraft parts create a dark and dramatic scene. The painting uses the wreckage of the planes to create an image that resembles a rough sea, giving the artwork its title and showing the effects of war and destruction.

About the Artist

Paul Nash was a British painter and war artist. He was known for his landscape paintings and works that showed the effects of war. His paintings often combined real places and objects with imaginative and symbolic elements.
