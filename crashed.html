"Crashed Aeroplane"

Painting Information

  • Artist: John Singer Sargent
  • Year Painted: 1918
  • Medium: Watercolor and graphite on paper
  • Art Movement: Impressionism
  • Location: Imperial War Museum, London

About the Painting

Crashed Aeroplane is a wartime painting by American artist John Singer Sargent. The painting depicts the remains of an aircraft after it crashed during World War I. Sargent created the work after witnessing the effects of the war while serving as a war artist.

About the Artist

John Singer Sargent was an American artist who became well known for his portraits and paintings of landscapes and people. He was also commissioned as a war artist during World War I, where he created works documenting his experiences and observations.
