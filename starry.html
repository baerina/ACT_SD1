"The Starry Night"

Painting Information

  • Artist: Vincent van Gogh
  • Year Painted: 1889
  • Medium: Oil on canvas
  • Art Movement: Post-Impressionism
  • Location: Museum of Modern Art, New York

About the Painting

The Starry Night is one of the most famous paintings by Dutch artist Vincent van Gogh. It was painted in 1889 while Van Gogh was staying at the Saint-Paul-de-Mausole asylum in Saint-Rémy-de-Provence, France. The painting shows a night sky filled with swirling stars, a bright moon, and a quiet village below. The movement of the sky and the use of strong colors make the painting easily recognizable.

About the Artist

Vincent van Gogh was a Dutch post-impressionist painter. He is known for his expressive paintings, strong use of color, and distinctive brushwork.
