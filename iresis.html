"Irises in Monet's Garden"

Painting Information

  • Artist: Claude Monet
  • Year Painted: 1900
  • Medium: Oil on canvas
  • Art Movement: Impressionism
  • Location: Musée d'Orsay, Paris

About the Painting

Irises in Monet's Garden is a painting by French Impressionist artist Claude Monet. The painting depicts a group of colorful irises growing in Monet's garden at his home in Giverny, France. The flowers are surrounded by greenery and natural light, creating a peaceful and vibrant garden scene.

About the Artist

Claude Monet was a French painter and one of the leading figures of the Impressionist movement. He was known for painting landscapes, gardens, and scenes that captured the effects of natural light and color.
